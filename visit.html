What to expect

Roughly how a Sunday goes

Worship first

We open by singing together. Some people raise their hands, some stand quietly with their coffee. Both are completely normal here.

Then the message

Pastor Nnenna teaches from the Bible - plainly, with the aim that you leave with something you can actually use on Monday.

Stay a while after

We do not rush out. If you want prayer, someone will pray with you. If you would rather slip away quietly, that is fine too.

Before you come

Questions people actually ask

What should I wear?
Whatever you own. Some of our family dress up because that is how they were raised to honour a Sunday; others come in jeans straight from a night shift. Nobody is counting. Come as you are - we mean that literally.
I have never been to church before. Will that be obvious?
Only to you. There is no secret handshake and no part of the service where visitors get asked to stand up. Sit wherever you like, join in with as much or as little as you want, and leave when you need to.
Can I bring my children?
Yes, always. We are a family church and children are part of the gathering, not an interruption to it. If you would like to know what is on for your child’s age group on a given Sunday, just ask us before you come.
Do I have to give money?
No. There is an opportunity to give during the service and information on our giving page, but visitors are under no obligation whatsoever. Please do not feel you need to put anything in.
What if I would rather watch online first?
That is a completely reasonable way to start, and plenty of our congregation did exactly that. Every service is streamed live and stays up afterwards - watch a few and come when you are ready.
Can someone pray with me?
Yes. Stay behind after the service and ask, or send us a prayer request any time. It stays between you, the pastor and God.
