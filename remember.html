No one in this church should be forgotten

A permanent home for the people we have lost, and words kept safe for the moments that have not arrived yet — through our partner, Saylavy.

Why we are doing this

Grief outlasts the funeral

A church shows up in the first two weeks. Then the casseroles stop, the cards stop, and the family is left holding everything on their own.

What tends to go missing first is not the big things. It is the sound of a voice. The way she told that one story. The blessing he said over the food every single Sunday for thirty years. Nobody thinks to record any of it, because nobody thinks they will need to.

So we have partnered with Saylavy to give our families somewhere to keep it. Not a social media page that a company can close, and not a folder of photos on a phone that will be wiped when the contract ends — something permanent, and something the family owns.

Precious in the sight of the Lord is the death of his faithful servants.

Psalm 116:15

This is for anyone

You do not have to be a member, and you do not have to have lost someone yet. Plenty of people set theirs up while everything is fine — which, honestly, is the right time to do it.

What it gives you

Four things, in one place

Saylavy calls itself a digital legacy app. In plain terms, it is somewhere to put the things you would be devastated to lose.

Memory Pages

A permanent home for a person — photos, video, their actual voice, the stories only your family knows. One page, kept for good, shared with whoever you choose.

Time Capsules

Words for a moment that has not arrived yet. A letter for a grandchild’s wedding, a message for a birthday twenty years out. Written now, delivered then.

Proof of Life

The app checks in with you now and then. While you answer, everything stays private. It only opens to the people you named when you stop answering.

Funeral Planning

The songs, the readings, the people to call. Decided quietly by you now, so your family is not guessing at it in the worst week of their lives.

Saylavy in your pocket

Have a look around it

This is the app, running right here in the page. Tap along the bottom to move between the screens — a memory page, a capsule waiting to be delivered, the check-in, and the plan.

Getting started

Three steps, and we will sit with you for all of them

If you are not confident with a phone, do not let that stop you. Bring it on a Sunday and someone will set the whole thing up with you after the service.

Download the app

Saylavy is on the App Store and Google Play, and works in a browser too.

Start with one thing

One page for one person, or one letter for one occasion. Do not try to do it all at once — nobody does.

Say who it is for

Name the people who should be able to open it. That is the part that matters most, and the part people forget.
