Welcome home

A LOVE community, rooted in grace

New Grace Victory Church is a vibrant community rooted in faith, unity and the transforming power of God’s grace. We are interdenominational and multicultural - a family drawn from many places, gathered around one table.

Whatever brought you here - curiosity, a hard season, a search for something real - you are welcome exactly as you are. No dress code, no background check, no pressure.

Our pastor

Pastor Nnenna Ogbonna

Pastor Nnenna leads New Grace Victory Church, teaching week by week from the pulpit in North York and to a congregation watching from far beyond it.

Her preaching returns again and again to the same ground the church is built on: that grace is not a reward for getting it right, and that the victory Christ won is already yours to walk in.
