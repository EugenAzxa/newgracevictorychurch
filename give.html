Giving, freely and gladly

Everything this church does - the room, the stream, the people it reaches - is carried by the generosity of ordinary people. Thank you.

Why we give

Not because God needs it

We give because everything we have was given to us first. Giving is how we say that out loud - and how a small church in North York keeps doing the work in front of it.

Practically, your giving keeps the lights on at Norfinch Drive, keeps every service streaming free to anyone anywhere in the world, and supports the people in our community who need a hand.

And if you are visiting or watching for the first time: please do not give. Sit down, be welcome, and let us give to you for a while.

Each of you should give what you have decided in your heart to give, not reluctantly or under compulsion, for God loves a cheerful giver.

2 Corinthians 9:7

Ways to give

However suits you

In person

There is an offering during every Sunday service at 465 Norfinch Drive. Cash or cheque, whatever you have.

Interac e-Transfer

From any Canadian bank account, send your gift to the address below. Add a message if you would like it directed somewhere specific.

giving@newgracevictorychurch.ca

Online

Give securely by card, once or on a schedule.
