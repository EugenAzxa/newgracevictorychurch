A family, not an audience

New Grace Victory Church is a vibrant LOVE community rooted in faith, unity and the transforming power of God’s grace - gathering in North York and online, wherever you are.

Our story

Interdenominational by conviction, multicultural by grace

We did not set out to build another denomination. We set out to make room.

Our church family is made up of people from many backgrounds, many countries and many church traditions. Some grew up in the pews. Some have never set foot in a church before. What holds us together is not a shared history - it is a shared need for grace, and a shared God who gives it freely.

We are people who know we are not perfect. But we are forgiven, cherished, and eternally loved by ABBA, our Heavenly Father. That is the ground we stand on, and it is the reason there is always room for one more.

Rooted in faith

The Bible read, taught and taken seriously - not as a rulebook, but as the word that makes people new.

Built on unity

Interdenominational by design. Whatever tradition shaped you, you will find a place at this table.

Open to everyone

Multicultural, multigenerational, and genuinely glad you came. Come as you are - we mean it.

Leadership

Pastor Nnenna Ogbonna

Pastor Nnenna leads New Grace Victory Church, teaching each week to a congregation gathered in North York and watching from far beyond it.

Her preaching keeps circling back to the same ground: that grace is not a reward for getting it right, that God’s love is not something you earn by behaving, and that the victory Christ has already won is yours to walk in - starting today, in the middle of whatever you are carrying.

Never forget: God loves you more than you can ever imagine.

What we believe

The things we hold in common

We are interdenominational, so there is a lot of room for difference here. These are the convictions we do not move on.

The Bible

God’s written word - trustworthy, living, and our final authority for what we believe and how we live.

One God

Father, Son and Holy Spirit: one God, eternally existing in three persons, perfect in love.

Jesus Christ

Fully God and fully human. He lived, was crucified, rose again, and is coming back. Everything turns on him.

Grace alone

Salvation is a gift, received by faith and never earned. Where sin increased, grace abounded all the more.

The Holy Spirit

God present with us now - comforting, convicting, gifting and empowering ordinary people for the work ahead.

The Church

One family across denominations, cultures and languages, called to love one another in a way the world can see.

Prayer

We pray expectantly - for healing, for breakthrough, and for the strength to get through an ordinary week.

The hope to come

Christ will return, and every wrong thing will be made right. We live now in the light of that certainty.

“For it is by grace you have been saved, through faith - and this is not from yourselves, it is the gift of God.”
Ephesians 2:8
